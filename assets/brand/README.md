# ParkRanger brand assets

ParkRanger's visual identity combines a classic ranger campaign hat with a
small DNS-record element. The hat represents stewardship and oversight; the
three gold pips represent the records kept in order for parked domains.

## Wordmark and tagline

The wordmark uses a restrained traditional serif treatment. Keep the capital
`P` and `R`; do not merge either letter into the symbol.

> Steward your parked domains.

Use the tagline in banners and supporting material, not inside the primary
logo or compact favicon.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of one gold
  pip.
- Use the standalone transparent logo for documentation, stickers, and other
  flexible layouts.
- Use the horizontal lockup when the project name must accompany the symbol.
- Use the supplied favicon assets at their native sizes; do not downscale the
  main logo in browser markup.
- Retain the supplied aspect ratios. Do not stretch, skew, recolor, outline, or
  add effects to the assets.
- Do not add conventional cybersecurity motifs such as shields, locks, radar,
  crosshairs, or hacker imagery.
- Do not present the identity as an insignia of a government agency.

## Provenance

The approved raster artwork was developed with OpenAI's built-in image
generator and finalized through iterative visual review. Standard favicon sizes
were deterministically derived from the approved favicon master.
